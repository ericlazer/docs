---
title: "Covenant Monitoring"
---

Covenants are predefined financial and operational rules that every borrower must follow. They define leverage, liquidity, reporting cadence, and drawdown limits. These are all encoded on-chain and enforced automatically by Nexio’s monitoring system, replacing manual OTC reporting, giving lenders continuous visibility, faster risk response, and verifiable audit trails.

### **Covenant Types**

Each borrower is bound by a customized covenant set based on its Series term sheet, borrower tier, and strategy type. All parameters are approved by Nexio’s Risk and Governance Committee before activation.

**Financial Covenants:**

- **Liquidity Ratio:** Borrowers must maintain minimum cash or collateral buffers relative to outstanding BTC credit.
- **Leverage Ratio:** Caps total exposure across venues to prevent over-leveraging.
- **Drawdown Limits:** Flags when portfolio value falls below tolerance levels, triggering margin checks or partial liquidations.

**Operational Covenants:**

- **Reporting Cadence:** Defines how often borrowers must submit verified financial or position data.
- **Venue Eligibility:** Restricts use of unapproved exchanges or counterparties to control venue risk.

### How Covenant Enforcement Works

Nexio’s monitoring engine continuously compares borrower metrics against their Series’ covenant parameters and enforces rules without manual input.

1. **Breach Detected:** The engine identifies when a borrower exceeds a covenant limit.
2. **Warning or Soft Halt:** A breach triggers a warning and pauses new draws until metrics return to compliance.
3. **Hard Halt and Recovery:** If unresolved within the defined cure period, the system freezes further activity and initiates collateral recovery.

All events, from breach to cure or liquidation, are logged on-chain and reflected in Series dashboards for full auditability.

### Example: Leverage Breach

If a borrower’s permitted leverage cap is 1.3× and exposure rises to 1.5×, Nexio immediately issues a warning and pauses draws.

If leverage normalizes within the 48-hour cure window, operations resume automatically. If not, the system escalates to a hard halt and triggers collateral liquidation.

Every step is immutable and visible to lenders through on-chain logs and Series-level monitoring dashboards.
