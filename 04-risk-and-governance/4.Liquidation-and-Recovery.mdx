---
title: "Liquidations & Recovery"
---

Liquidations protect lenders when a borrower’s position breaches covenants or collateral thresholds.

Instead of manual intervention or an OTC call, the system executes predefined recovery logic directly on-chain. Losses, if any, remain isolated to the affected Series, while all other Series continue operating normally to reduce any systemic risk.

For instance, if a borrower fails to maintain collateralization requirements or misses a coupon payment, the Series’ liquidation module activates automatically: freezing borrower allocations, valuing collateral, and initiating recovery through approved exchange venues.

### **How It Works**

Each liquidation follows five standardized steps:

1. **Trigger**: A covenant breach (e.g., under-collateralization, missed coupon, or hard-halt) activates the liquidation module for that Series.
2. **Freeze and Price:** The borrower’s Series allocation is frozen. The pricing engine retrieves reference prices from approved exchanges to determine the collateral's market value.
3. **Liquidate Collateral to BTC**: The collateral is liquidated and converted to BTC through whitelisted venues or counterparties. All proceeds are returned directly to the Series vault.
4. **Record Recovery:** The vault records total recovered BTC, realized costs, and any shortfall. These figures automatically update Series metrics (NAV, coupon pool, loss counters).
5. **Report and Audit:** Every liquidation event emits on-chain logs with timestamps and recovery ratios, allowing lenders, auditors, and governance to verify results in real time.

The liquidation sequence is entirely automated and visible on-chain, meaning the lender can verify what occurred and when.

To see how this process functions in practice, consider the following example.

### Example: BTC Liquidation

Suppose a Tier 2 borrower’s collateral ratio falls below the required 120% threshold following a sharp market decline. In that case, Nexio’s risk engine automatically freezes the borrower’s Series allocation and retrieves live pricing data from approved oracles.

The system then liquidates collateral through whitelisted venues, converting proceeds directly back into BTC for the Series vault. In this example, 9.8 BTC is recovered from a 10 BTC exposure, with the 0.2 BTC shortfall automatically recorded in the Series’ loss counter and reflected in real-time dashboards.

No manual intervention occurs. Instead, everything is executed and logged on-chain for lenders and auditors to verify.
