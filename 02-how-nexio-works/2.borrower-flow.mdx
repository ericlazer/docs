Nexio borrowers include institutional trading firms, market makers, arbitrage desks, and more seeking fixed-rate BTC-denominated credit for short- and medium-term strategies. These borrowers are KYC/KYB-verified entities that already operate under regulatory oversight and manage large, hedged positions across exchanges.

Although some traditional BTC lending markets exist, they entail borrowing USD or stablecoins and converting to BTC. This introduces FOREX friction, timing risk, and rate volatility. Nexio provides native, fixed-rate BTC loans that operate under enforceable legal agreements, transparent collateral policies, and continuous on-chain monitoring.

Each borrower operates within predefined credit tiers (Tier 1–3), determining their collateral ratios, reporting cadence, and borrowing limits. Borrowers don’t create Series; they draw BTC from Series that match their approved risk tier and trading strategy.

Benefits include:

- **BTC stays on Bitcoin**: custody and settlement never leave the Bitcoin network.
- **Institutional-grade trust**: Every Series is governed by legal agreements (Master Loan, Pledge, Term Sheet) and KYC/KYB verification.
- **Predictable, fixed-rate structure**: Yields and borrowing costs are locked in from origination, not variable like DeFi.
- **Transparent, auditable operations**: All utilization, collateral, and covenant data are visible on chain.
- **Compliant, scalable infrastructure**: Bridging regulated credit standards with on-chain automation.

Now let's take at how this looks in practice.

### **Step-by-Step Process**

The following shows the example flow, with an example at the end to help solidify.

1. **Verification: **Borrowers complete institutional onboarding (KYC/KYB), sign the Master Loan Agreement, and are assigned a credit tier (1 to 3) based on collateral quality and reporting cadence.
2. **Proposal Submission: **A borrower submits a draw request referencing an active Series compatible with their tier and strategy.
3. **Risk Validation:** The Operator Queue checks that the proposal complies with all Series rules — utilization caps, collateral thresholds, and concentration limits — before authorizing.
4. **BTC Disbursement:** Once validated, Nexio’s signers co-authorize a Bitcoin transaction from the Series’ Taproot vault to the borrower’s registered Bitcoin address.
   - This BTC remains **native and fully usable** on the Bitcoin network.
   - Borrowers can deploy funds for approved trading or hedging activities.
   - These addresses are **whitelisted and monitored**, ensuring all activity remains within Nexio’s enforceable perimeter.
5. **Coupon Payments & Repayment:** Borrowers pay coupons and repay principal per the Series schedule. All transactions are recorded on-chain, updating Series utilization, covenant status, and lender dashboards in real time.
