Lending on Nexio is designed for institutions, funds, and high-net-worth BTC treasuries seeking predictable, fixed-rate yield without giving up Bitcoin custody.\
Lenders don’t create Series themselves — they allocate BTC into pre-defined Series that have published term sheets, fixed coupons, and clear borrower eligibility.

Each Series functions like a structured loan fund for Bitcoin: lenders supply capital, verified borrowers draw under enforceable terms, and coupon payments flow back on schedule. All activity (e.g. deposits, coupons, or repayments) is visible on-chain.

### **Why lenders use Nexio instead of DeFi or OTC lending**

Lending on Nexio has several advantages over traditional BTC lending, including:

- **Predictable income:** Fixed-rate Series for consistent BTC-denominated returns.
- **Institutional counterparties:** All borrowers are KYC/KYB verified and operate under enforceable legal frameworks.
- **Custody on Bitcoin:** BTC never leaves the Bitcoin network or Nexio’s audited Taproot vaults.
- **On-chain transparency:** Utilization, collateralization, and repayments are visible in real time.

Here's what this looks like in application.
