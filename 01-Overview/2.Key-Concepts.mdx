## Core Concepts

Nexio bridges traditional credit systems with Bitcoin’s security model. To understand how it works, we’ll cover the building blocks below before going into greater depth in subsequent sections.

### **1. Series**

A **Series** is a ring-fenced BTC lending pool with fixed terms, similar to a fixed-term fund in traditional finance.

When lenders deposit BTC into a Series, that BTC is used only for one specific lending strategy. Each Series has its own vault, interest rate, duration, and risk limits. For example, one Series might focus on a 90-day fixed-rate loan to trading firms engaged in market-making.

Each Series has:

- A defined strategy (e.g., CME basis, delta-neutral, market-making)
- A fixed interest rate and duration
- Named, **KYC/KYB-verified borrowers**
- On-chain visibility into utilization, collateralization, and covenant status

When lenders deposit BTC into a Series, their funds are used only within that Series. No cross-mixing or rehypothecation occurs.

### **2. uBTC (Series Token)**

When BTC is deposited into a Series, the depositor receives **uBTC**, an on-chain receipt that represents proportional ownership in that Series and its yield. This can be thought of as like a Certificate of Deposit (CD).

The uBTC balance increases in value as the Series earns yield, and it is burned at redemption to release BTC from the vault. Because uBTC minting and burning are linked to verified Bitcoin transactions, the supply of uBTC always matches the BTC actually held in custody.

<Expandable title="Example: Lending 10 BTC into a Series">
  Suppose a lender deposits 10 BTC into a Series offering a 7% fixed coupon over 90 days.

  Upon confirmation of the Bitcoin transaction, Nexio mints **uBTC** representing that lender’s proportional share of the Series. If the total Series pool is 100 BTC, the lender’s uBTC represents 10% ownership (10 BTC of the 100 BTC).

  Borrowers pay interest into the Series, causing its balance to grow, for instance, from 100 BTC to 103 BTC. The uBTC share price automatically reflects this increase.

  At maturity, the lender burns their uBTC on chain, and Nexio’s vault releases the corresponding 103 BTC directly back to their registered Bitcoin address.
</Expandable>

### 3. **Master Vault and yBTC**

The Master Vault acts as a meta-layer that automatically allocates pooled BTC across multiple Series. Rather than lending or borrowing to an individual Series, participants can deposit once into the Master Vault and receive yBTC.

Each Series deploys its allocated BTC into its respective strategy and, upon completion, returns the principal plus yield to the Master Vault. The vault aggregates these returns and updates the value of yBTC accordingly. As yields from the underlying Series accrue, yBTC’s redemption value automatically increases, giving holders diversified exposure without the need to manage multiple positions.

### **4. Vaults and Custody**

All BTC remains secured within **Taproot Vaults** on the Bitcoin network, which refer to specialized Bitcoin addresses that enforce programmable spending conditions. Each Series has its own Taproot Vault, which function like segregated custodial trust accounts that hold client assets separately.

They're controlled by threshold-signature schemes, ensuring that no single operator can move funds unilaterally.

As each Series has its own dedicated vault, all collateral and activity is segregated from other Series or the Master Vault. Each vault also encodes reclaim and emergency paths, which are time-locked backup routes that allow funds to be safely recovered if operators or infrastructure fail.

### **5. Legal Layer**

There are three types of signed, enforceable contracts that back every borrower and Series:

- **The Master Loan Agreement (MLA):** The MLA is a standing contract between Nexio and each approved borrower. It defines the legal framework for all future lending activity — including repayment obligations, event-of-default rules, and dispute resolution.
- **The Pledge Agreement:** This agreement defines the borrower’s collateral obligations. It specifies what assets are pledged, how they are held in custody, and the process for liquidation or recovery if a default occurs.
- **Series Term Sheets:** The Term Sheet applies to one specific Series. It lists the coupon rate, duration, collateral requirements, and covenants that govern that particular BTC lending program.

The hash of each respective document is stored on-chain, meaning that anyone can verify that a loan is operating under approved legal terms.

### **How It All Fits Together**

- The **vault** is the safe that holds the BTC.
- The **Series** is the specific loan or investment program.
- The **uBTC** is your proof of deposit into that Series.
- The **Master Vault** combines multiple Series like an index fund.
- The **yBTC** is your diversified share of that fund.
- The **legal layer** defines everyone’s rights and obligations.
