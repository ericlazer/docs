Each Series on Nexio balances yield potential with borrower tier, collateralization, and operational complexity.

Lower-tier Series offer stable, fully hedged returns; higher-tier Series offer greater yield in exchange for modest market or operational exposure.

![Screenshot 2025-10-30 at 12.52.20 AM.png](/images/Screenshot2025-10-30at12.52.20AM.png)

Each Series balances yield with risk and borrower sophistication:

- **CME Basis** offers stable, regulated returns with minimal exposure.
- **Delta-Neutral Funding** provides a higher yield through cross-exchange funding spreads while staying mostly hedged.
- **Market-Making** offers the highest potential return but carries active trading risk.

All Series remain BTC-backed, proof-gated, and transparently monitored on chain.
