title: "Strategy 1: CME-Basis"
---

The CME Basis strategy captures the **price premium between spot Bitcoin and regulated Bitcoin futures** listed on the Chicago Mercantile Exchange (CME). The **CME** is a major U.S. derivatives exchange where institutions trade regulated Bitcoin futures and other financial contracts.

On Nexio, this strategy is reserved for **Tier 1 borrowers** (regulated trading firms with direct CME access, proven risk controls, and strong collateralization) given its low directional risk and reliance on institutional infrastructure.

Institutional desks exploit this spread by buying BTC on the spot market and selling a corresponding CME futures contract that trades at a higher price. As the futures contract approaches expiry, the two prices converge — producing a predictable return independent of BTC’s direction.

This is a **market-neutral trade**, meaning it doesn’t rely on whether Bitcoin rises or falls — only on the closing of that spread.

### **Example: 3-Month CME Futures**

If spot BTC trades at \$100,000 and the 3-month CME future trades at \$106,000, a borrower can:

1. Buy **1 BTC** on the spot market.
2. Sell **1 CME futures contract** at \$106,000.

When the contract expires, the futures price converges with spot. The borrower delivers the BTC against the futures position and keeps the \$6,000 premium, equivalent to roughly 6% annualized yield.

### **Why It Works**

CME futures often trade at a premium due to institutional demand, capital constraints, and USD funding costs. By simultaneously holding offsetting long and short positions, borrowers eliminate price exposure and profit purely from the convergence of prices over time.

For lenders, CME Basis Series provide predictable, low-volatility returns derived from regulated market spreads, with BTC-backed coupons verifiably paid on-chain.
