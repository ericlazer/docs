All BTC within Nexio stays in [**Taproot Vaults**](https://learnmeabitcoin.com/technical/upgrades/taproot/): specialized Bitcoin addresses controlled by **threshold-signature (Schnorr)** schemes, an advanced multi-party cryptographic (MPC) signing scheme.

When a lender deposit, their BTC moves directly into a **Series vault** (if they select a strategy) or into the **Master Vault** (if they delegate). The BTC never leaves Bitcoin custody; what moves on-chain are proofs and mirrored tokens (uBTC or yBTC).

This design eliminates single-point control: no individual or operator can move funds unilaterally. Every transaction requires multiple independent signers to co-authorize it (the Schnorr signature covered below), creating institutional-grade security directly on Bitcoin. Each Series is assigned its own dedicated vault, keeping capital isolated and preventing any cross-series exposure or rehypothecation.

<Expandable title="How Taproot Vaults & MPC Encryption Work">
  **Taproot Vaults**

  Taproot is a Bitcoin upgrade that lets complex spending conditions be encoded compactly inside a single address. In Nexio’s case, each Series vault is a Taproot tree that includes multiple possible paths for spending BTC:

  - **Normal Path:** Used for standard borrower draws or lender redemptions.
  - **Reclaim Path:** Allows recovery after a defined timeout if operations halt.
  - **Emergency Path:** Enables a 3-of-3 multisig to secure funds in case of infrastructure compromise.

  **Schnorr Threshold Signatures**

  Traditional Bitcoin multisig requires each signer to publish a separate signature, making transactions heavier and more traceable.

  Schnorr signatures, introduced with Taproot, allow multiple parties to jointly produce one aggregated signature that looks identical to a single-signer transaction.
</Expandable>

#### **Schnorr Signatures**
